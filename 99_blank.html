Preparation of 3D printer


Any FDM type 3D printer with a printable area of at least 240(W) x 240(D) x 200(H) mm should works. As of 2026, the following printers are recommended:

- Bambu Lab P2S
- Bambu Lab A1

There are many videos for the setup and tutorial of these printers on Youtube.








【Extending of nozzle lifespan】

Removing of dust from the filament using a filament cleaner like this (Kimwipe wrapped around the filament and secured with tape) can extend the nozzle's lifespan several times.









【Preventing print object from peeling off during printing】
